---

## 📊 Overview

The **Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI project** designed to analyze the sales performance, customer behavior, and revenue trends of a retail business across multiple e-commerce platforms.  

This Power BI project explores the story behind retail sales data transforming numbers into insights.  
The dashboard reveals key trends in **sales performance**, **revenue growth**, and **customer demographics**, helping decision-makers identify profitable channels, boost customer retention, and optimize business growth.

## 🧠 Key Insights

- **Total Revenue:** 21M  
- **Total Orders:** 31K  
- **Unique Customers:** 28K  
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 682.07  
- **Average Customer Age:** 39.5 years  

### ✳️ Highlights
- **Amazon** and **Myntra** are the top-performing sales channels.  
- **Quarter 1** shows the highest sales across all time periods.  
- **Men (64.05%)** contribute more revenue than **women (35.95%)**.  
- **92.64%** of customers are one-time buyers — indicating a strong retention opportunity.  
- **Sarees, Kurtas, and Bottoms** are the top-selling product categories.  
- **March** recorded the highest customer engagement, 18.84% higher than December.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Customer Retention:**  
   Launch loyalty programs or targeted offers to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers.  

2. **Channel Optimization:**  
   Increase marketing investment in high-performing platforms like Amazon and Myntra.  

3. **Inventory Management:**  
   Maintain optimal stock for popular product categories and preferred sizes (L, M).  

4. **Regional Growth:**  
   Expand campaigns in underperforming regions identified in the map visual.  

5. **Promotion Planning:**  
   Use seasonal and quarterly trends to plan discount and marketing campaigns effectively.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.  
2. Open `Apec_store_dashboard.pbix` in **Power BI Desktop** (Download: [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/)).  
3. Explore all pages — *Sales Analysis*, *Revenue Analysis*, *Customer Analysis*, and *Insights*.  
4. Use filters (Age, Category, Size, Channel) to interact with the data.  
5. Access the live hosted version using the Power BI link above.
